MulTaBench is a benchmark for multimodal tabular learning with text and images. It balances tasks across modalities and evaluates pairs of a tabular model and an encoding method, alongside end-to-end (E2E) models that read raw text themselves. The leaderboard's purpose is to drive smarter encoding methods. Two are on it today: Frozen, which embeds text with e5-small and images with DINOv3-small and projects each column to 30 dimensions with PCA, and TAR, target-aware representations, which naively fine-tunes the encoder on the training labels as a preprocessing step before the same projection.

Elo vs cost

Each entry's Elo, under the filters above, against its median train + predict time per 1,000 rows (log scale), TAR's fine-tuning included, optionally with an estimate of the encoders' time on those rows. ★ marks the Pareto front: no other model is both faster and stronger.

Cost

Median time per 1,000 rows over all successful runs, on the hardware each model is assigned. Runs read the cached embeddings, so this leaves out encoding; the text and image columns estimate it from the cached encoders' own time, and the toggles above add it to the total. Fine-tune is the mean time TAR's LoRA fine-tuning took per 1,000 training rows, on an H100, and always counts.
