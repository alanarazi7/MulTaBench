MulTaBench is a benchmark for multimodal tabular learning with text and images. Its purpose is to drive smarter encoding methods, towards our vision of truly Multimodal Tabular Foundation Models. Its 80 datasets balance image and text modalities, classification and regression, and how hard the text or images are to encode, with the Core slice focusing on the 40 datasets where frozen embeddings are not enough.

Pareto Frontier

Each entry's Elo, under the filters above, against its median train + predict time per 1,000 rows (log scale), TAR's fine-tuning included; With encoding time adds an estimate of the encoders' time on those rows. The times come from runs and caches on different machines, so the comparison is an approximation, but where encoding counts it dominates the cost. ★ marks the Pareto front: no other model is both faster and stronger.

Cost

Median time per 1,000 rows over all successful runs, on the hardware each model is assigned. Runs read the cached embeddings, so this leaves out encoding; the text and image columns estimate it from the cached encoders' own time, and With encoding time adds it to the total. Fine-tune is the mean time TAR's LoRA fine-tuning took per 1,000 training rows, on an H100, and always counts.
